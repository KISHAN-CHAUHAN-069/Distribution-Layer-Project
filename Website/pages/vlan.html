VLAN Segmentation & IP Addressing Scheme

Detailed matrix of virtual local area networks, subnet gateways, and interactive lookup tools.

VLAN ID: 10

Students VLAN

Allocated for student computer labs and personal devices. High host density with internet access permissions, isolated from administrative databases.

Subnet: 192.168.10.0/24
Gateway SVI: 192.168.10.1
Host Range: .2 to .254
VLAN ID: 20

Faculty VLAN

Dedicated to academic staff and professor workstations. Allowed inter-VLAN routing access to grading systems and academic department portals.

Subnet: 192.168.20.0/24
Gateway SVI: 192.168.20.1
Host Range: .2 to .254
VLAN ID: 30

Administration VLAN

Restricted high-security subnet hosting financial databases and administrative servers. Protected by upcoming Distribution Layer ACL policies.

Subnet: 192.168.30.0/24
Gateway SVI: 192.168.30.1
Host Range: .2 to .254
